# Uzbekistan — 7-day winter trip

## Trip brief
- **Dates assumed:** 10–17 January 2027; fixed-date example, with the original ±2-day flexibility retained.
- **Route:** Amsterdam → Tashkent → Samarkand → Bukhara → Tashkent → Amsterdam.
- **Party:** 2 adults + 1 infant (age 0).
- **Style:** Premium; convenient flights, central/high-quality hotels, private transfers where useful.
- **Important assumption:** The outbound flight arrives at 01:40 on 11 January, so the Tashkent room should be booked from 10 January to guarantee early-morning access.

## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1 — Sunday 10 January: Depart Amsterdam
- 09:40 flight from Schiphol.
- Keep the evening light; the next segment lands after midnight.

### Day 2 — Monday 11 January: Arrive and gentle Tashkent
- 01:40 arrival at Tashkent International.
- Pre-arrange an official hotel transfer and request a cot/crib and early-room access.
- Sleep and recover.
- Afternoon: Khast Imam complex; dress modestly.
- Late afternoon: Chorsu Bazaar and the old-city streets.
- Dinner in central Tashkent; keep the evening short with an infant.

### Day 3 — Tuesday 12 January: Tashkent → Samarkand
- Use the official Uzbekistan Railways e-ticket portal to select an Afrosiyob service. Uzbekistan Railways’ official material describes Tashkent–Samarkand as about 2h15m; a separate official itinerary example shows an 08:00 departure and 10:08 arrival, but the January 2027 timetable must be checked in the portal.
- Afternoon: Gur-e-Amir Mausoleum and nearby Ruhabad area.
- Sunset/early evening: Registan Square exterior and illuminated views if operating.
- Overnight in Samarkand.

### Day 4 — Wednesday 13 January: Samarkand monuments
- Morning: Registan Ensemble; official winter hours are 08:00–20:00 daily from 20 November to 20 February.
- Midday: Bibi-Khanym Mosque and Siab Bazaar for bread, dried fruit, nuts and sweets.
- Afternoon: Shah-i-Zinda necropolis and Afrasiab Museum/archaeological area if open.
- Keep the walking route flexible: winter temperatures and uneven stone surfaces make a short taxi between clusters worthwhile with a stroller.

### Day 5 — Thursday 14 January: Samarkand → Bukhara
- Check the official rail portal for the Samarkand–Bukhara service and current seats/classes; exact January timings were not exposed in the official search result.
- Afternoon in Bukhara old city: Lyabi-Hauz, trading domes and the Poi-Kalyan area.
- Evening: dinner around Lyabi-Hauz.

### Day 6 — Friday 15 January: Bukhara → Tashkent
- Morning: Ark Citadel, Bolo Hauz Mosque and the Samanid Mausoleum area.
- Official tourism listings give Ark Citadel typical hours of 09:00–18:00 Monday–Friday; verify locally because winter/holiday hours can change.
- If the rail timetable permits, take a daytime Bukhara–Tashkent train; otherwise use a private transfer to the airport/rail station and return to Tashkent.
- Check in to the Tashkent hotel for the final night.

### Day 7 — Saturday 16 January: Tashkent buffer and return
- Use this as a relaxed museum/coffee morning, shopping, or recovery buffer.
- For the fixed flight, leave the hotel around 02:30–03:00 for the 05:55 Tashkent departure.
- 05:55 TAS → IST → AMS; scheduled arrival 13:05.

## Getting around
- **Cities:** walking plus pre-booked hotel/private taxis; public transport is useful in Tashkent, but a stroller and winter weather favour short taxi hops.
- **Between cities:** prefer Afrosiyob/official Uzbekistan Railways where the January schedule fits. Book business/first-class seats if available and reserve infant-friendly seating.
- **Bukhara/Tashkent final leg:** do not leave this to the last day without a buffer; the 05:55 international flight makes the final Tashkent night essential.
- **Airport transfers:** arrange through the hotel or a reputable official taxi app; avoid accepting unsolicited airport offers.

## Tickets and reservations
- **Registan:** official electronic ticketing is available at https://tickets.registon.uz/; January hours are 08:00–20:00 daily. The official result did not expose a current January 2027 ticket price.
- **Gur-e-Amir, Shah-i-Zinda, Bibi-Khanym, Ark and Chor Minor:** official pages/search results did not expose reliable January 2027 prices for all sites. Plan to buy at official entrances or verify with the local visitor desk shortly before travel.

## Winter and entry notes
- Official Uzbekistan Travel guidance describes winter conditions ranging roughly from +12°C to −15°C depending on region and recommends warm layers. Pack a warm coat, hat, gloves, waterproof shoes and stroller rain/wind protection.
- The official Uzbekistan e-visa portal is https://e-visa.gov.uz/. Visa eligibility for a Dutch passport, infant documentation, passport validity, and any visa-free rule should be checked directly for January 2027; do not rely on the older embassy page returned in search.
