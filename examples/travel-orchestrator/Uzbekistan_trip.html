🗓️ Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 · Sun 10 Jan · Depart Amsterdam

    Fly out of Schiphol

    • 09:40 flight from Schiphol (economy option).
    • Keep the evening light; the next segment lands after midnight.
  2. Day 2 · Mon 11 Jan · Arrive + gentle Tashkent

    Land, recover, old city

    • 01:40 arrival at Tashkent International — pre-arranged official hotel transfer; request cot/crib + early-room access.
    • Sleep and recover.
    • Afternoon: Khast Imam complex (dress modestly).
    • Late afternoon: Chorsu Bazaar and old-city streets.
    • Dinner in central Tashkent; keep the evening short with an infant.
  3. Day 3 · Tue 12 Jan · Tashkent → Samarkand

    Afrosiyob rail day

    • Book via the official Uzbekistan Railways e-ticket portal; Tashkent–Samarkand is about 2h15m (official example: 08:00 → 10:08 — verify Jan 2027 times in the portal).
    • Afternoon: Gur-e-Amir Mausoleum + Ruhabad area.
    • Sunset/evening: Registan Square exterior and illuminated views if operating.
    • Overnight in Samarkand.
  4. Day 4 · Wed 13 Jan · Samarkand monuments

    Registan, mosques, necropolis

    • Morning: Registan Ensemble — official winter hours 08:00–20:00 daily (20 Nov–20 Feb).
    • Midday: Bibi-Khanym Mosque + Siab Bazaar (bread, dried fruit, nuts, sweets).
    • Afternoon: Shah-i-Zinda necropolis + Afrasiab Museum/archaeological area if open.
    • Keep walking flexible — winter + uneven stone; short taxi hops worthwhile with a stroller.
  5. Day 5 · Thu 14 Jan · Samarkand → Bukhara

    Rail to Bukhara, old-city evening

    • Check the official rail portal for Samarkand–Bukhara services, seats and classes (exact January timings not exposed in search).
    • Afternoon: Lyabi-Hauz, trading domes, Poi-Kalyan area.
    • Evening: dinner around Lyabi-Hauz — atmospheric, walkable from central old-city hotels.
  6. Day 6 · Fri 15 Jan · Bukhara → Tashkent

    Citadel morning, return to Tashkent

    • Morning: Ark Citadel, Bolo Hauz Mosque, Samanid Mausoleum area. Ark typical hours Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 — verify locally (winter/holiday hours change).
    • Daytime Bukhara–Tashkent train if the timetable fits; otherwise private transfer to station/airport and return to Tashkent.
    • Check in to the Tashkent hotel for the final night.
  7. Day 7 · Sat 16–17 Jan · Buffer + return

    Buffer day, night departure

    • Buffer day: relaxed museum/coffee morning, shopping, or recovery if a train was delayed.
    • Leave the hotel around 02:30–03:00 for the 05:55 Tashkent departure.
    • 05:55 TAS → IST → AMS; scheduled arrival 13:05.

🚆 Getting around

  • Cities: walking + pre-booked hotel/private taxis; public transport is useful in Tashkent, but a stroller + winter weather favour short taxi hops.
  • Between cities: prefer Afrosiyob / official Uzbekistan Railways where the January schedule fits. Book business/first-class seats if available; reserve infant-friendly seating. Timetable: official schedule page.
  • Final leg: do not leave Bukhara/Tashkent to the last day without a buffer — the 05:55 international flight makes the final Tashkent night essential.
  • Airport transfers: arrange through the hotel or a reputable official taxi app; avoid unsolicited airport offers.
